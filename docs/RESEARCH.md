# Research contract

Production computes full exact Bitcoin SHA-256d. Reduced-round, symbolic,
carry, SAT and algebra experiments are research tools. Midstate reuse,
unrolling, Boolean identities, occupancy and batching are ordinary engineering.

Measure throughput H, measured energy E or null, algorithmic scaling evidence
alpha, economic collapse Rc, exact rejection rho, Q=-log10(1-rho), analysis
cost F relative to conventional hashing, memory M and usable family K.
Pruning effective speed is 1/[F+(1-rho)*phi], including construction, conversion,
proof, splitting, survivor hashing and host time. UNKNOWN retains the family.

Preserve diverse species: scalar IR, unrolled IR, bit planes, carry-save,
shared DAG, BDD, SAT, hybrid target and structural families. Retain negative
evidence and runnable frontier even when ordinary independent hashing wins.
Kill repeated explosive or economically dominated regions rather than merely
raising memory budgets. No finite search guarantees a SHA shortcut exists.

Current historical results and boundaries remain in `results/` and `STATUS.md`.
New results must identify exact source/configuration, seed and total measured
cost. Small-family parity and launch-amortized slopes cannot establish sublinear
algorithmic work. Pool acceptance is separate from synthetic correctness and
from BTC payout. Power is never inferred from TDP.
